Suffa Dars is a traditional Islamic palli dars (mosque-based learning) system conducted under the Alathurpadi Dars in Melmuri, Malappuram. It is part of the classical method of Islamic education where students study religious subjects such as Qur'an, Hadith, Fiqh, and Arabic under the guidance of scholars. The system follows the traditional dars style, focusing on spiritual development, discipline, and deep Islamic knowledge within a mosque environment.
